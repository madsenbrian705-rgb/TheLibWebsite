Ch. 01 — The Library

Tell your parents you were at the library.

It wouldn't even be a lie. Since 2011 The Library has been where Western comes to eat, drink and blow off steam: trivia on Mondays, every game on the TVs, the patio under the string lights, and a dance floor that doesn't quit until close.

The kitchen keeps up all night with double smash Buster Burgers, hand-tossed pizza, wings in thirteen sauces and lake perch baskets, plus drafts cold enough to get you through finals.
